Writing Task 1 Blueprint

The 4-Paragraph Report (150 - 200 Words)

1. المقدمة: إعادة صياغة السؤال مع ذكر الوحدات.
2. النظرة العامة (The Mandatory Overview): رصد الاتجاه العام الكلي وأعلى وأدنى نقطة دون ذكر أرقام إطلاقاً (بدونها تقف درجتك عند Band 5.0 تلقائياً).
3. فقرة التفاصيل الأولى (Body 1): تجميع البيانات المتشابهة والمقارنات الدقيقة بالأرقام.
4. فقرة التفاصيل الثانية (Body 2): بقية البيانات ومقارنات التقاطع.
الوقت المخصص: 20 دقيقة بدقة صارمة.

Writing Task 2 Blueprint

The TEXS / P.E.E.L Essay Structure (265 - 285 Words)

1. مقدمة BRO: Background (خلفية موضوعية) ➔ Rephrase (إعادة صياغة) ➔ Opinion (الموقف الصريح).
2. جسم 1 (TEXS): Topic Sentence (الفكرة المركزية) ➔ Explanation (الشرح بـ In other words) ➔ eXample (مثال دولي مقنع) ➔ Significance (جملة Cleft متقدمة).
3. جسم 2 (TEXS): مناقشة الفكرة الثانية بتعليل ومثال وجملة Inversion.
4. الخاتمة (Summary + Recommendation): تلخيص الفكرتين دون أي أفكار جديدة.
الوقت المخصص: 40 دقيقة (3 تخطيط + 32 كتابة + 5 مراجعة PSTA).

نموذج مقال التربية (IELTS Essay 1: Parenting Courses - Band 8.5)

📄 فتح ملف المقال الأصلي PDF

المقدمة (BRO): (R) In the modern era, whilst some couples opine that parental education programs are pivotal for rearing children, opponents argue that they are unnecessary because it is an innate skill. (O) However, in my opinion, these workshops are quintessential to nurturing psychologically stable youths.

جسم 1 (TEXS): (T) To embark on, parenting classes play an imperative role because they develop communication skills between mothers and fathers and their infants. (E) This means that if parents attend professional discipline workshops, these classes will bridge the generation gap between guardians and their dependents... (X) An eminent example is that Denmark and Sweden have been ranked as the best places in the world... (S) As a result, what can be said is that [cleft] parenting skills have a considerable influence on most aspects of a child's development.

جسم 2 (TEXS): (T) Moreover, these sessions help parents to know how to safeguard their sons and daughters from the detrimental effects of cultural impacts and social media. (E) In other words, when custodians attend such courses, they will know how to curtail the risk of child abuse... (X) For example, a research study conducted by Washington University emphasizes... (S) Thus, not only is caregiver training pivotal [inversion], but also it is crucial for parent-child interactions.

الخاتمة: (S) In conclusion, by highlighting the above-mentioned points, it can be reiterated that educational upbringing programs have beneficial consequences... (O) Moreover, definitely the authorities should obligate parents to join such programs before they plan to beget a newborn.
